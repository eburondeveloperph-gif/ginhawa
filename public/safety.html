SAFETY CHARTER & INTEGRITY CONTROLS

Ginhawa Safety, Boundaries & Compliance

Professional touch requires absolute physical safety, strict professional boundaries, and transparent reporting channels for clients and therapists alike.

Zero Misconduct Policy

Strict zero-tolerance for any sexual solicitation, non-clinical touch, harassment, or verbal abuse from either client or therapist. Immediate account suspension upon verified report.

DOH & PRC Verification

Only therapists with verified Department of Health (DOH) massage licenses, PRC physical therapy registration, or TESDA NC-II certificates are permitted to accept sessions.

Real-Time Dispatch Check-in

Home service appointments require dual GPS arrival and completion check-ins, allowing our 24/7 Safety Dispatch to monitor session timelines.

Therapist Welfare Fund

₱50 accumulated per qualifying session builds the therapist's medical, personal accident, and professional liability insurance coverage (target ₱3,500/cycle).
