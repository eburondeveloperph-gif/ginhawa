The Transparent ₱1,000 Fee Breakdown

Unlike exploitative booking platforms, Ginhawa publishes its exact fee reconciliation. Here is how an illustrative ₱1,000 booking is divided to empower therapists:

Component Share / Amount
Customer Gross Payment ₱1,000.00
Payment Gateway Processing (2%) - ₱20.00

Why We Protect Therapist Earnings

High-quality therapy requires energized, well-compensated, and insured practitioners.

  • Zero hidden deduction penalties.
  • Direct payouts transferred weekly via InstaPay or GCash.
  • Accumulated Welfare Fund pays for medical & liability coverage.
  • Audited double-entry financial ledger prevents payment disputes.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

Up to 2 hours before appointment: 100% free reschedule or full credit refund.

Within 2 hours of home appointment: A ₱250 therapist travel stipend is retained if the practitioner is already en route.

Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)

Your address, phone number, and medical screening answers are encrypted and revealed to your assigned therapist only 1 hour prior to session start.

Personal health details are strictly confidential and automatically archived after service completion.
